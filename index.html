Meet the maker

From a little candle kit to a beautiful dream.

In 2019, while pregnant and unable to work, Serrell discovered a candle-making kit at Hobby Lobby. With about $88, a few extra jars and a love for beautiful fragrance, she started making candles for her baby shower.

What began as a creative solution became Shiner Scents — a collection inspired by warmth, self-expression and the joy of sharing something handmade.

With love, Serrell

Love your candle

Care for your glow

Trim your wick to ¼ inch before each burn. Allow wax to melt evenly across the surface when practical. Never leave a burning candle unattended, and keep away from children, pets and flammable materials. Follow the instructions included with your candle.
